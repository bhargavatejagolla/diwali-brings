DEDICATED TO

DHARJI SHRAVYA

"In the warmest corner of this celebration,
some people naturally bring more light than a thousand lamps."

Dear Shravya,

Diwali has always been about the quiet lights we bring into each other’s lives. Thinking back on this year, having you as a friend has been one of its most genuine and comforting blessings.

May your days be filled with the kind of happiness that arrives unexpectedly, the kind of laughter that stays a little longer, and the kind of peace that makes everything feel right.

May every little light you see tonight remind you of how appreciated and rare you are. Wishing you and your loved ones a celebration as radiant and wonderful as you are.

With all warmth and light
